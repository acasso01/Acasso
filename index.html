About Anthony Casso

IT support professional with a passion for keeping systems running and people productive.

Hi, I'm Anthony Casso — an IT support and systems coordination professional based in Tucson, Arizona. I have over four years of hands-on experience supporting Microsoft 365 environments, managing Active Directory accounts and permissions, troubleshooting network infrastructure, and maintaining HIPAA compliance in a regulated healthcare setting.

Most recently I served as Office Manager and IT Coordinator at Aspen Dental, where I was the on-site point of contact for system issues across multiple locations — handling everything from new hire onboarding in Active Directory and M365 to switch resets, Ethernet cabling, and vendor escalations.

I also run TechBoys GVS, a local tech support business serving the Southern Arizona community, where I help home and small business clients with networking, device troubleshooting, and security setup.

I hold a B.S. in Information Technology (Cybersecurity) from Capella University, am currently pursuing my M.S. in IT, and am working toward my CompTIA Network+ certification. I'm actively looking for help desk, desktop support, or IT coordinator opportunities where I can bring real-world experience and a get-it-done attitude.
